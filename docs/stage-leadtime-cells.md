# Who fills the stage-by-lead cells? The plants behind U and V at 13-24 months

Status: description, not a scored check (no predictions were stated). Follow-up to the twenty-second result ([`stage-leadtime.md`](stage-leadtime.md)). MV2 of Munim, Inc. (made with AI agents). Code Apache-2.0; text and tables CC BY 4.0.

## What this is

The twenty-second result scored EIA-860M planned operating months by stage and lead band. It said that which generators make up the U and V cells at 13-24 months "has not been read". This note reads them, beside the U and V cells at 0-3 months for comparison. U is under construction and half or less complete; V is under construction and more than half complete. As there, a row is one generator in one report month, and MW is summed over rows, so a generator listed in many reports weighs more. Job `cellmakeup-v1` (20 October 2026; 3 GB) fetched eia860m_slip.py (commit 329b7e6) and leadtime.py (commit 9ee085b), rebuilt leadtime.py's rows on PUDL's EIA-860M changelog (reports to 2026-08, report months 2016-01 to 2023-06), and ran [`../tools/annual-flows-candidate/cellmakeup.py`](../tools/annual-flows-candidate/cellmakeup.py) 0.1.0. Before reading anything it checked (K1) that all four cells match the published ones in rows, generators, MW and on-time share; K1 passed for all four. Raw output: [`stage-leadtime-cells-output.md`](stage-leadtime-cells-output.md), [`../data/stage_leadtime_cells.json`](../data/stage_leadtime_cells.json). Shares are shown to three decimals from the job's four-decimal values.

## What we found

**V at 13-24 months is a handful of projects, above all Vogtle.** The cell has 79 generators at 51 plants and 57,078.8 MW over rows. Its ten largest generators hold 0.654 of that MW and its ten largest plants 0.764. One plant, Vogtle (GA, nuclear), holds 0.312: unit 4 alone is 0.293, from 15 reports that listed it as more than half built with its planned month 13-24 months away. Vogtle, Washington Parish Energy Center (LA, gas combined cycle, 0.138) and Lincoln Combustion (NC, gas combustion turbine, 0.085) together hold 0.535. None of the three was on time for any of its MW in this cell. By technology the cell is gas combined cycle 0.313, nuclear 0.312 and solar 0.244.

**So V's low on-time share at 13-24 months (0.139) is mostly a statement about a few large projects.** Without Vogtle's rows the cell would be on time for about 0.20 of its MW, and without Vogtle and Washington Parish about 0.25. Both are still far below V at 0-3 months (0.409). Vogtle's rows in V at 0-3 months are at most 5,091.0 MW (it is not among that cell's ten largest plants), and taking them out could move that cell's share by less than 0.005. So the twenty-second result's L2 verdict does not rest on Vogtle: V's fall from 0-3 to 13-24 months would be between 0.20 and 0.22 without it (0.2699 with it), against 0.0068 for early-stage plans.

**U at 13-24 months is broad.** It has 506 generators at 315 plants and 636,740.1 MW over rows; its ten largest generators hold 0.223 of it, its ten largest plants 0.308 and its largest plant, again Vogtle, 0.055. Gas combined cycle is 0.461 of the MW (on time for 0.365 of it) and onshore wind 0.227 (on time for 0.122). The largest generator is Goodnight (TX, onshore wind, 500.0 MW), listed 73 times as under construction with its planned month 13-24 months away, with planned months from 2017-10 to 2023-06 and an operating month of 2024-04; it is 0.055 of the cell by itself. Without Vogtle's rows the U cell would be on time for about 0.26 of its MW, still below every shorter U band as published (lowest 0.285).

**Many of the largest not-yet-online plans have no operating month under their own key.** Five of the ten largest U generators at 13-24 months (South Field Energy, Chilocco Wind Farm, Alle-Catt Wind Energy, Easter and Canisteo Wind Farm) and four of the ten largest V generators (the three at Washington Parish Energy Center, and Rugged Solar) have no operating month under their plant and generator id in any report to 2026-08. Some of these may have been built under other generator ids. One lead: South Field Energy (OH) is planned as one 1,105.0 MW generator in U at 13-24 months, and the same plant id is among the ten largest plants of V at 0-3 months with 4 generators. A plan re-reported under new generator ids would be read as not online under the old key; rule R, which links re-keys only when the generator id stays the same, cannot catch a split like that. This was not checked here and is not a claim.

For anyone reading EIA-860M: a share computed over a thin cell can be mostly one or two projects. For plants more than half built with a planned month over a year away, the survey's records are dominated by a few large, long-delayed projects; for plants under construction and half or less complete, the same lead holds a broad mix. These are descriptions of the survey's own records, not causes.

## Correction to the twenty-second result

The twenty-second result says: "The cell behind L1's failure for V at 13-24 months is the thinnest: 79 generators, 315 rows." The cell is the thinnest, but it is not behind L1's failure for V. L1 said that the on-time share would never rise from one band to the next. For V it rises from 0-3 to 4-6 months (0.4088 to 0.4253), and the 13-24 cell is V's lowest, which is consistent with L1. For U the rise is from 4-6 to 7-12 months (0.2848 to 0.3220). L1's verdict (failed) is unchanged. The note now carries a dated correction; its scored text is left as it was.

## Limits

MW is summed over rows, so long-listed generators weigh more; that is how the twenty-second result weighted them too, and the count-based share there (0.206 on time for V at 13-24 months) shows the other weighting. The sensitivity shares above are computed from four-decimal shares and rounded to two decimals; they drop whole plants after the fact and are not predictions. Plant names and states are from each generator's latest record. Generator and plant ids are EIA's; re-keyed plants are not linked. The 0-3 month cells are read only as far as their ten largest generators and plants. Only the four cells named here were read.
